# BURNRATE 0.6.4

- **Ultra-compact floating corner pill.** Shrink to tab now renders as a `40 px` capsule with today's live `$` spend, your most urgent limit, a `+N` badge, and an in-place `↻` refresh button. Hovering smoothly unfurls an overview card with burn rate, cache hit %, at-risk limits, and an expandable summary of remaining healthy and balance providers.
- **Faster desktop scanning and per-plan ROI.** Adds a sticky daily-spend header with `Limits | Spend | Plans` navigation, a two-column grid for calm providers, a docked bottom utility bar, and ROI progress bars on each configured plan.
- **Smooth transitions and polish.** Adds 120 Hz eased hover card reveal/retract and cohesive spring transitions across segmented tabs, cards, fuel bars, and controls (respecting reduced motion).
- **Hardening and reliability fixes.** Integrates the 0.6.0 audit remediation (`BR-01`–`BR-42`) across collectors, sync, profiles, and plans, separates ended plans into history, and keeps removed profiles hidden cleanly.
