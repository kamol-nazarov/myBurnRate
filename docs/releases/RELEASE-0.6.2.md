# BURNRATE 0.6.2

- **Edit plans in the desktop app.** Open **Settings → Plans** or choose **Manage** on the Plans card.
  Add a plan, schedule a price change, record its last active day, or preview and confirm a correction.
  On a paired computer, sign in to your hub to manage the plans saved there.
- **Stop tracking a source.** Disabled sources disappear from the live readings for that computer.
  Recorded usage stays in analytics. To stop counting a saved plan's cost, set its last active day in Plans.
